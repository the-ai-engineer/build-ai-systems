# Remote Working Policy

This policy covers regular home working, office attendance, temporary location changes, and working outside the United Kingdom.

Employees may work remotely for up to three days each week when their role supports it.

The normal working pattern must be agreed with the employee's manager.
Teams may require office attendance for planning, training, customer meetings, or other business needs.
Employees should remain available during their agreed working hours and follow the information-security rules used in the office.

Occasional work from another UK location requires advance manager agreement.
Working from another country is not permitted unless HR, Tax, and Information Security approve it in writing before travel is booked.
An approved remote arrangement does not change the employee's contractual work location.

The policy assistant can explain the general policy but cannot decide whether an individual role or location is eligible.

# Expenses Policy

This policy covers receipts, deadlines, approval, meals, travel, and accommodation for business expenses.

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are necessary for their work.
Normal travel between home and an employee's usual workplace is not a business expense.

Every claim must include a receipt and a short business reason.
Claims should be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
Expenses above £250 require manager approval before purchase.

Rail travel should use a standard-class fare.
Flights under six hours should use economy class.
Hotel limits are £175 per night in London and £140 per night elsewhere, including taxes.
An employee who chooses a personal upgrade must pay the difference.

When an overnight stay is approved, employees may claim up to £25 for an evening meal.
Alcohol, minibar items, parking fines, and personal entertainment are not reimbursed.
Foreign-currency claims should use the amount shown on the employee's card statement or the HR system's rate for cash purchases.

The policy assistant can explain the rules but cannot approve travel, decide whether a cost was reasonable, or submit an expense claim.

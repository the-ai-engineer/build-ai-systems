# Bereavement and Compassionate Leave Policy

This policy covers paid bereavement leave, compassionate leave, notification, and additional support after a death or serious family emergency.

Employees may take up to five paid working days following the death of an immediate family member.
Immediate family includes a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or a person who lived in the employee's household.
Employees may take one paid working day to attend the funeral of another person who was significant to them.

Employees should tell their manager as soon as reasonably possible and may ask HR to handle the conversation privately.
The company does not require personal details to be posted in Slack.

A manager and HR may agree up to five additional days as annual leave, unpaid leave, or a temporary change to working hours.
Employees can also use the confidential employee assistance service described on the benefits portal.

The policy assistant can explain the standard allowance and process but cannot judge a relationship, approve leave, or decide an individual exception.

# Annual Leave Policy

This policy covers annual leave allowance, public holidays, requests, and carrying unused leave forward.

The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave each holiday year, in addition to public holidays.
Part-time allowances are calculated on a pro-rata basis by HR.

Employees should submit requests through the company HR system before making non-refundable travel plans.
Requests for five or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least four weeks in advance.
Managers consider team cover and business needs when reviewing requests.

Employees may carry up to five unused days into the next holiday year with manager approval.
Carried leave must be used within the first three months of the new holiday year.
Unused leave above that limit is not normally carried forward unless HR confirms an exception.

The policy assistant can explain the general rules but cannot approve leave, calculate a part-time allowance, confirm an exception, or read an employee's remaining balance.

# Employee Data and Records Policy

This policy covers how employees view or update their personal details, who may access employee records, and how to raise a privacy request.

Employees can update their home address, emergency contact, and telephone number in the company HR system.
Changes to legal name, right-to-work documents, or bank details require the verification steps shown in the HR system.

Access to employee records is limited to people who need the information for an approved business purpose.
Managers can see only the records needed to manage their team.
Medical, payroll, and formal case records have additional access restrictions.

Employees who want a copy of their records or who believe information is inaccurate should contact the Privacy team through the private HR portal.
Passwords, identity documents, bank details, medical information, and complete employee records must never be posted in the public HR Slack channel.

The policy assistant can explain the process but cannot access, display, correct, or delete an employee record.

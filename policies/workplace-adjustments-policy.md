# Workplace Adjustments and Accessibility Policy

This policy covers requests for changes to equipment, workspace, duties, communication, or working arrangements because of a disability or health condition.

Employees can start a request with their manager or through the private HR portal.
They should describe the barrier they are experiencing and the change that might help.
They do not need to share medical details in the public HR Slack channel.

HR will discuss the request with the employee and may ask for an occupational health assessment when more information is needed.
Temporary adjustments can be agreed while a longer-term request is reviewed.
Examples include specialist equipment, changes to communication format, adjusted hours, or changes to the physical workspace.

Every request is considered individually with the employee.
Managers should review agreed adjustments when the role, workplace, or employee's needs change.

The policy assistant can explain the process and give general examples but cannot decide what is appropriate, assess medical information, or confirm that a request will be approved.

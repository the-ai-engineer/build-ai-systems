# Working Hours and Time Off in Lieu Policy

This policy covers standard working hours, core collaboration time, breaks, overtime approval, and time off in lieu.

The standard full-time working week is 37.5 hours, excluding meal breaks.
Unless another pattern is agreed, employees should be available for core collaboration time from 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone on normal working days.

Employees should take a meal break of at least 30 minutes when working more than six hours.
Managers and employees should plan workloads so regularly working beyond contracted hours is not expected.

Overtime or weekend work must be approved by a manager before it is worked.
Where time off in lieu is agreed, the employee records the extra hours in the HR system and should use the time within 60 days.
Payment for overtime depends on the employee's contract and must be confirmed by HR or Payroll.

The policy assistant can explain the standard pattern and recording process but cannot read a timesheet, approve overtime, or interpret an individual contract.

# Flexible Working Policy

This policy covers requests to change working hours, working patterns, or contractual work location on a temporary or permanent basis.

Employees should submit a flexible working request through the company HR system.
The request should describe the proposed arrangement, the preferred start date, whether it is temporary or permanent, and any expected effect on the team.

The manager and HR will discuss the request with the employee and aim to give a written decision within six weeks.
They may agree a trial period of up to 12 weeks before confirming a permanent change.
Approval depends on the role, customer needs, team cover, security, and the practical effect of the arrangement.

A regular home-working pattern must also follow the Remote Working Policy.
A change needed because of a health condition or disability should use the Workplace Adjustments Policy instead of including medical details in the flexible working request.

The policy assistant can explain the process but cannot predict the decision, approve a request, or decide whether an adjustment is reasonable.

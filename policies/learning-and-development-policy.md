# Learning and Development Policy

This policy covers the annual learning budget, professional memberships, study time, and approval for courses or conferences.

Each employee has a learning budget of up to £1,000 per holiday year for development relevant to their current role or an agreed career plan.
Unused budget does not carry into the next holiday year.

Employees need manager approval before booking a course, examination, conference, or professional membership.
The request should state the learning objective, total cost, time away from normal work, and how the learning will be shared with the team.
Travel and accommodation follow the Expenses Policy and are included when considering the total cost.

Employees may use up to three paid study days each holiday year for an approved qualification or examination.
The company may pay for one relevant professional membership within the annual budget.

The policy assistant can explain the standard budget and process but cannot approve spending, confirm remaining budget, or decide whether an activity is relevant.

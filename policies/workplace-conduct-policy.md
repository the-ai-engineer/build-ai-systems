# Workplace Conduct, Bullying, and Grievance Policy

This policy covers expected conduct, bullying, harassment, discrimination, informal concerns, formal grievances, and confidential reporting.

Everyone must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and respect.
Bullying, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, and deliberately humiliating behaviour are not accepted in person or through digital channels.

An employee with a general concern may speak to their manager or HR.
If the concern involves their manager, they may contact HR directly through the private HR portal.
A formal grievance should be submitted privately in writing and should describe the concern, relevant dates, and the outcome the employee is seeking.

HR decides how a concern is handled, who needs to know, and whether an investigation is required.
Information is shared only with people who need it for a fair process, but complete confidentiality cannot be promised.
An immediate safety risk should be reported to local emergency services or workplace security, then to HR when safe.

The policy assistant can explain reporting routes but must not collect case details, assess allegations, identify a likely outcome, or conduct an investigation.

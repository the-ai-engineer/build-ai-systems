# Sickness Absence Policy

This policy covers reporting sickness, medical evidence, return-to-work conversations, appointments, and support for longer absences.

Employees who are too unwell to work should tell their manager before their normal start time when reasonably possible.
They should give an expected return date if known, but they do not need to share a diagnosis in a public channel.

For an absence of seven calendar days or fewer, employees complete the company's self-certification form when they return.
For a longer absence, HR will explain what medical evidence is needed and how to provide it privately.

The manager holds a short return-to-work conversation after every sickness absence.
Its purpose is to confirm that the employee is fit to return, identify support, and correct the absence record.
Repeated or long-term absence is handled privately with HR and may involve the Workplace Adjustments Policy.

Routine medical appointments should be arranged outside working hours where practical or agreed with the manager in advance.
Emergency treatment should never be delayed to obtain approval.

The policy assistant can explain the reporting process but cannot assess fitness for work, request a diagnosis, interpret medical evidence, or decide pay or attendance action.

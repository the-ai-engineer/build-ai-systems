# Onboarding and Probation Policy

This policy covers induction, required training, probation reviews, equipment, and support during an employee's first months.

New employees receive an induction plan during their first week.
It includes role expectations, security and data-protection training, workplace safety, payroll checks, and introductions to key colleagues.
Required training should be completed within 30 days unless the course owner gives a different deadline.

The standard probation period is three months.
Managers hold check-ins around weeks four, eight, and twelve to discuss progress, feedback, goals, and support.
HR may confirm an extension of up to three months when more time is needed to assess agreed objectives.

Equipment needed for the role is ordered by the hiring manager.
New employees should report missing access or equipment through the IT service desk rather than buying a replacement themselves.

The policy assistant can explain the timetable and process but cannot assess performance, confirm that probation has passed, or change an employee's start details.

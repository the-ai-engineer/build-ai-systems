# Pay and Benefits Policy

This policy covers the monthly pay date, payslips, general benefit information, bonus decisions, and where to raise a personal pay query.

Employees are paid monthly on the last working day of the month.
Digital payslips are normally available in the company HR system two working days before payday.
If the usual payday is affected by a bank holiday, Payroll publishes the revised date on the HR portal.

The benefits portal lists the current health, wellbeing, pension, and insurance options available to each employment group.
Some benefits begin after probation and some have separate enrolment windows.
The portal and the employee's written enrolment confirmation are the authority for individual coverage.

Any company bonus is discretionary unless an employee's contract says otherwise.
Managers cannot promise a bonus outside the approved review process.

A missing payment, unexpected deduction, tax question, or individual benefit decision must be raised privately with Payroll or HR.
The policy assistant can explain general dates and routes but cannot access payroll, calculate pay, interpret a payslip, or confirm personal benefit eligibility.

# Family Leave Policy

This policy covers the company process for maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental, neonatal care, and unpaid parental leave.

Employees should tell HR and their manager about planned family leave as early as they reasonably can.
The HR system contains one private request form for every type of family leave.
The form asks for the expected dates, the type of leave, and any documents needed for that request.

HR confirms the employee's eligibility, pay, dates, and keeping-in-touch arrangements in writing.
These details depend on the employee's circumstances, contract, length of service, and applicable statutory rules.
Employees can ask for a return-to-work conversation before their leave ends.

Managers must not assume that an employee's career plans have changed because they take family leave.
Requests for a temporary change to working hours should follow the Flexible Working Policy.

The policy assistant can explain the request process and types of leave but cannot calculate entitlement, confirm pay, approve dates, or ask for private family or medical details.
